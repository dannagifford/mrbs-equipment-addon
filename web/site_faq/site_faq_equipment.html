Booking equipment

Select an equipment type, such as Microscopes, then select an individual equipment item, such as Microscope 1. Click an available calendar slot, enter a description and the start and end times, then save the booking. Each item represents one physical piece of equipment.

Booking several items or repeating a booking

Where your administrator permits it, use Control-Click to select several equipment items in the booking form. Choose a repeat pattern to make a recurring booking. Review any conflicts reported before saving.

Changing or cancelling a booking

Open the booking in the calendar and choose Edit Entry or Delete Entry. For repeating bookings, choose whether to change a single entry or the whole series. Your account permissions, approval status and booking policies determine which actions are available.

Equipment types and booking types

An equipment type groups similar items, for example Microscopes or Centrifuges. A booking type describes the purpose or category of a reservation, for example Internal or External. These are separate settings.

Adding equipment

Administrators can open Equipment, add an equipment type, then add an equipment item within it. Use a unique, recognisable name for each item. Configure the equipment type's opening hours, booking limits, approvals and notification addresses as required.

Unavailable equipment

Administrators can disable an equipment item or an entire equipment type to prevent new bookings. Existing reservations remain recorded and can still be found in Search and Report results.

Availability and capacity

Overlapping reservations for an equipment item are checked by the booking system. The Capacity field is optional descriptive information; it does not represent a stock quantity. Add each bookable physical item separately.
